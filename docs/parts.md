## What it costs

Most of these parts come in packs of 5, 10 or 100, so the price per robot depends on how many robots you build.

- **A class or a group, packs split across three or more robots: about $87 per robot** from Amazon.
- **Same, with the servos and boards from AliExpress: about $55 per robot.** Allow 2 to 4 weeks for shipping.
- **One robot, every Amazon pack bought once: about $235.** You end up with most of a second robot's small parts left over, so find a friend to build one too.
- **Name-brand US vendors only (Adafruit, Pololu, DigiKey): about $170 per robot.** Two Pololu 5 V 5 A regulators alone are $66. Use these as the fallback when one part from a cheap pack turns out bad.

Prices change. Check each listing before you order.

## The one expensive component

The 2S LiPo kit, two batteries and a charger, at about $17. A rolling wheel cannot trail a power cable, so the battery has to ride on the robot, and eight servos need a pack that can deliver 5 A.

## Why MG90S and not SG90

The plastic-gear SG90 is about $1 cheaper per servo. In the closed wheel the servos push against each other continuously, and plastic gears strip. The whole robot costs less than two smart servos, so there is room for metal gears.

## Tools and consumables

Not counted in the per-robot price:

- FDM printer with a 180 mm bed, or a print service (about $20 to $30 for the set)
- Soldering iron and solder, flush cutters, small Phillips screwdriver, 2.5 mm hex key, calipers
- Multimeter, to check both 5 V rails before anything is plugged in
- Kitchen scale, to measure the magnet pull-off force
- Two USB-C data cables
- LiPo-safe bag or a metal tray for charging
- Superglue, hot glue, double-sided foam tape
