Ouro, the worm that bites its tail and rolls away

A flat strap of nine links lies on the desk. It crawls like a caterpillar, rolls itself up from both ends, snaps its head onto its tail with magnets, and becomes a wheel. Tap it and it bursts open. Everything below is a replay of the MuJoCo simulation, not a drawing of what it might do.

MuJoCo renders of the same run. The strap rolls up tips first, so no servo ever lifts more than two links.

What it is made of

Top view, lying flat. Every joint has the same axis, so the whole robot bends in one plane and the 80 mm width keeps it from falling over.
